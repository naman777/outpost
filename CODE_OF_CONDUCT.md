# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and other project spaces.
Discuss the work and explain disagreements with evidence. Welcome contributors with
different backgrounds and experience levels.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual content directed at participants,
and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict
participation. Decisions should reflect the severity and context of the behavior.
Report concerns privately to the maintainer at namankundra6@gmail.com. Include links
and context, and avoid sharing another person's private information unnecessarily.
